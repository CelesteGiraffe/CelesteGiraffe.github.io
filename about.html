Game Systems

AI & Gameplay Mechanics

I like building games where enemies react intelligibly and player controls feel immediate and satisfying.

  • Finite state machines for enemy AI (patrol, investigate, attack, stagger) with NavMesh navigation.
  • Responsive movement controllers using coroutine input buffers and tight dash mechanics.
  • Clean separation between gameplay logic, data structures, and visual rendering.

Usability & Design

Accessibility & Player Psychology

My psychology background taught me to think carefully about player ergonomics, cognitive friction, and accessibility.

  • Input abstraction supporting controller remapping and alternate device bindings.
  • High-contrast readable UI, clean typography, and WCAG AA compliance across web tools.
  • Clear visual and audio feedback so mechanics are intuitive to pick up.
